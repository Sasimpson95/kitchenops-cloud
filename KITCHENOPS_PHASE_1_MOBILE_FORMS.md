# KitchenOps Phase 1 — Mobile-First Forms

This build extends Android Preview 2 and converts the highest-use operational forms into full-screen mobile sheets while preserving desktop modals.

Updated forms:
- Recipes (already mobile-native in Preview 2)
- Products
- Suppliers
- Purchase Orders
- Receive Invoice
- Start Stocktake
- Stock Transfers
- Waste

On phones the forms now:
- use the full available screen;
- respect Android safe areas;
- keep action buttons accessible at the bottom;
- use 16px form text to avoid unwanted browser zoom;
- provide larger touch targets;
- keep desktop modal behaviour on larger screens.
